San Andreas Uniform Code of Military Justice 圣安地列斯统一军事司法法典

San Andreas Uniform Code of MilitaryJustice (SA-UCMJ) 圣安地列斯统一军事司法法典（SA-UCMJ）

General Provisions 总则

This chapter defines the official name, applicable scope, basic legal principles, commander-in-chief authority and independence of military judiciary of SA-UCMJ. 本章规定了《圣安地列斯统一军事司法法典》的正式名称、适用范围、基本法律原则、最高统帅权限以及军事司法机关的独立性。

  • The code applies to all members of San Andreas National Guard and state militia 本法典适用于圣安地列斯国民警卫队及州民兵所有现役人员
  • Military justice shall follow presumption of innocence and due process 军事司法遵循无罪推定原则与正当程序原则
  • The Governor serves as Commander-in-Chief of all state armed forces 州长为本州全部武装力量的最高统帅
  • Military courts exercise judicial power independently without improper interference 军事法院依法独立行使审判权，不受非法干预

Military Justice Organization 军事司法组织

Specifies the establishment, duties, independence and legal authority of the Office of the Military Prosecutor. 本章规定军事检察院的设立、法定职责、独立地位与法定职权。

  • Military prosecutors are nominated by Governor and confirmed by state legislature 军事检察官由州长提名、州议会审议任命
  • Responsible for investigation, evidence collection and public prosecution in military courts 负责案件侦查、证据固定，并向军事法院提起公诉
  • Prosecutors enjoy independent law enforcement power with strict accountability rules 检察官依法独立行使执法权，并受严格责任约束
  • Authorized to issue subpoenas, seize evidence and coordinate cross-jurisdiction cases 有权签发传票、扣押物证，并协调跨司法管辖案件办理

Military Offenses & Punishments 军事违法行为与处罚

Classifies military violations into three tiers and sets clear penalties for all statutory military crimes. 本章将军事违法行为划分为三个等级，并对各类法定军事罪名设置明确处罚标准。

  • Three offense tiers: minor, moderate and severe with corresponding courts 违法行为分为轻微、一般、严重三个等级，由对应层级军事法庭审理
  • Covers disobedience, AWOL, desertion, confidentiality breach, corruption and more 涵盖拒不服从命令、擅离岗位、逃兵、泄密、职务贪腐等各类罪名
  • Penalties include detention, fines, rank reduction and long-term military confinement 处罚种类包含拘禁、罚金、军衔降级、长期军事监禁
  • Dual jurisdiction applies if service members commit ordinary civilian crimes 现役人员触犯普通民事犯罪时，适用军民双重司法管辖规则

Military Justice Procedure 军事司法程序

Establishes three-level military court system, procedural rights of defendants and standardized evidence rules. 本章设立三级军事法院体系，明确被告人各项诉讼权利以及标准化证据采信规则。

  • Three-tier court system: summary, special and general military courts 三级法院体系：简易军事法庭、专门军事法庭、普通军事法庭
  • Defendants retain legal counsel, trial, confrontation and appeal rights 被告人享有委托律师、庭审质证、当庭抗辩以及上诉的权利
  • Military personnel must record all official operations with body cameras 军人执行公务必须佩戴执法记录仪全程留存操作影像
  • Prosecutors must comply with Brady Rule to disclose favorable evidence 检察官必须遵循布雷迪规则，主动提交对被告人有利的证据材料

Administrative Disciplinary Measures 行政惩戒措施

Covers non-judicial disciplinary sanctions for minor misconduct and permanent separation penalties. 本章规定轻微违纪行为的非司法类惩戒方式，以及永久除名类处罚措施。

  • Minor sanctions: warning, extra duty, restriction, fines and rank demotion 轻微处罚：警告、额外勤务、行动限制、罚金、军衔降级
  • Sanctions shall not violate basic rest and legal rights of members 所有惩戒措施不得侵犯队员基本休息权与法定人身权利
  • Severe repeated violations may result in involuntary discharge or dishonorable separation 多次严重违纪可予以强制退役、不名誉除名处理
  • Discharged personnel are permanently banned from state military organizations 被除名人员将永久禁止加入本州各类军事相关组织

Supplementary Provisions 附则

Regulates coordination with civilian laws, governor's pardon authority and code effective standards. 本章规范本法典与民事法律的衔接规则、州长赦免权限以及法典生效适用标准。

  • Clarifies jurisdiction coordination between military and civilian courts 明确军事法庭与民事法院之间的司法管辖衔接规则
  • The Governor holds supervision, pardon and sentence commutation power 州长拥有执法监督、赦免以及刑罚减刑的法定权限
  • All official decisions of clemency must be issued in written form 所有官方赦免类决定必须以书面形式正式下达
  • This code supersedes all prior inconsistent military regulatory documents 本法典效力优先于所有此前发布的冲突类军事规章制度

The team reserves the right to take appropriate action against violations. 团队保留对各类违规行为采取相应处置措施的权利。

Serious violations may result in expulsion from the team! 严重违规行为团队将直接予以开除处理！
